# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Policy owner: Finance

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Brightline Analytics will reimburse, the limits that apply, and how to submit a claim. It applies to all employees and contractors who spend their own money on approved company business.

## 2. General Rules

- Submit expense claims through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense.
- Attach an itemized receipt for every expense of $25 or more.
- Each claim must include the business purpose of the expense.
- Expenses above the limits in this policy need written approval from your manager before they are incurred.
- Claims are reviewed by your manager and then by Finance. Approved claims are paid within 10 business days.

## 3. Expense Rules

### EXP-201 Meals While Traveling

When traveling overnight for business, employees may claim meals up to $75 per day. Itemized receipts are required. Alcohol is not reimbursable under this rule.

### EXP-202 Client Meals

Meals with clients or prospective clients are reimbursable up to $120 per person, including the employee. The claim must list the names and companies of all attendees and the business discussed.

### EXP-203 Team Meals

Team meals for celebrations or working sessions are reimbursable up to $40 per person. The most senior employee present must pay and submit the claim, and the manager must approve the meal in advance.

### EXP-204 Lodging

Hotel stays are reimbursable up to $250 per night in standard cities and up to $350 per night in high-cost cities listed on the finance portal. Lodging must be booked through the company travel portal unless Finance approves an exception. Room upgrades are not reimbursable.

### EXP-205 Mileage

Employees who drive a personal vehicle for business are reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. The claim must include the start and end locations, the distance, and the business purpose. Normal commuting between home and the office is not reimbursable.

### EXP-206 Per Diem for Extended Travel

For business trips longer than 5 consecutive nights, employees may choose a flat per diem of $60 per day instead of claiming meals under EXP-201. No meal receipts are needed when the per diem is used, but the employee cannot combine the per diem and EXP-201 on the same trip.

### EXP-207 Client Entertainment

Client entertainment, such as event tickets or activities, is reimbursable up to $200 per person. It requires written approval from a Director before the event. Entertainment where alcohol is the main activity is not reimbursable.

### EXP-208 Home Internet and Phone

Employees with an approved remote or hybrid arrangement under the Remote Work Policy may claim up to $50 per month for home internet and mobile phone costs combined. Submit one claim per month with the bill attached.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed:

- Personal entertainment, such as movies or in-flight purchases
- Traffic tickets, parking fines, and towing charges
- Spouse, partner, or family travel costs
- Gym memberships and personal grooming
- Lost or stolen personal property

## 5. Misuse of This Policy

Submitting false or inflated claims is treated as a serious conduct issue and may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Finance audits a sample of claims every quarter.

## 6. Questions

Questions about this policy should be sent to the Finance team through the finance portal.
