# Remote Work Policy

Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Policy owner: People Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees at Brightline Analytics may work remotely, what the company expects from remote workers, and what support the company provides. Its goal is to give employees flexibility while keeping collaboration, security, and service quality high. Where this policy conflicts with local employment law, the local law applies.

## 2. Eligibility (RW-101)

Remote work is available to employees whose role can be performed effectively outside the office. Eligibility is decided by the employee's manager together with People Operations.

- Employees must have completed their 90-day introductory period before working remotely on a regular basis.
- Roles that require on-site equipment, in-person customer support, or physical security duties are not eligible for regular remote work.
- Employees on a performance improvement plan may have remote work paused until the plan is completed.

## 3. Work Arrangements (RW-102)

The company offers two remote work arrangements:

- **Hybrid:** the employee works remotely up to 3 days per week and in the office on the remaining days. The team agrees on shared in-office days each quarter.
- **Fully remote:** the employee works remotely every day. A fully remote arrangement requires written approval from a Vice President and is reviewed every 12 months.

All remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they work their contracted hours and meet team deadlines.

## 4. Home Office Setup and Equipment (RW-103)

The company provides each approved remote employee with a laptop, one external monitor, a headset, and a keyboard and mouse. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends or the remote arrangement stops.

Approved remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for furniture or accessories such as a desk or chair. Monthly home internet and phone costs are reimbursed under rule EXP-208 of the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

Employees are responsible for keeping a safe, quiet workspace with a reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download speed.

## 5. Security and Data Protection (RW-104)

Remote employees must protect company and customer data at all times:

- Always connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN, and never share devices with family members or other people.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.
- Do not print confidential documents at home unless your manager approves it, and shred any printed copies after use.
- Report a lost or stolen device to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.

Breaking these rules may lead to remote work being withdrawn and to disciplinary action.

## 6. Communication and Availability (RW-105)

Remote employees are expected to stay as reachable as colleagues in the office. During core hours, respond to messages from your team within 2 business hours. Keep your calendar up to date, including time off and focus blocks. Cameras are recommended for team meetings and required for interviews and client meetings.

## 7. Working From Another Location

Employees may work temporarily from a location other than their usual home address for up to 20 working days per year, with manager approval. Working from another country requires approval from People Operations and Legal at least 30 days in advance, because of tax and immigration rules.

## 8. Changing or Ending an Arrangement

Either the employee or the company may ask to change or end a remote arrangement. The company will normally give at least 30 days' notice before requiring an employee to return to the office, unless there is a performance or security concern.

## 9. Questions

Questions about this policy should be sent to People Operations through the HR portal.
