# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Policy owner: People Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees at Brightline Analytics earn, request, and use paid time off (PTO). PTO can be used for vacation, personal matters, or any other reason. The company encourages every employee to take regular time away from work to rest and recharge.

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for PTO from their first day of employment. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week earn PTO on a prorated basis, based on their scheduled hours. Contractors and temporary workers are not eligible for PTO under this policy.

## 3. PTO Accrual

Full-time employees earn 20 days of PTO per year. PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month, starting on the employee's first day. New employees may use PTO as soon as it has accrued.

PTO does not accrue during unpaid leaves of absence. Employees cannot take PTO they have not yet accrued, unless their manager and People Operations approve an advance of up to 3 days.

## 4. Requesting PTO

- Submit all PTO requests through the HR portal.
- For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance.
- For single days, submit the request at least 1 business day in advance.
- Managers should respond to requests within 3 business days.

Managers may decline a request during critical business periods, such as quarter-end close, but should work with the employee to find other dates.

PTO may be taken in full days or half days. Employees who are unexpectedly unable to work, for example because of a family emergency, should tell their manager as soon as possible and record the absence in the HR portal within 2 business days of returning. Unrecorded absences may be treated as unpaid leave.

## 5. Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next year. Any unused PTO above 5 days is forfeited at the end of the year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year, or they are forfeited.

People Operations sends a reminder in October to employees who have more than 5 unused days, so they can plan time off before the end of the year.

## 6. Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Full-time employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave per year for their own illness or to care for a family member. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out when employment ends. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required.

## 7. Company Holidays

The company observes 10 paid holidays each year. The holiday calendar is published on the HR portal every December. Company holidays do not count against an employee's PTO balance. If a holiday falls during an employee's approved PTO, that day is not deducted from their balance.

Employees who are required to work on a company holiday because of business needs will receive a replacement day off, to be taken within 60 days. The replacement day must be agreed with the employee's manager and recorded in the HR portal.

## 8. PTO When Employment Ends

When an employee leaves the company, any accrued but unused PTO is paid out in their final paycheck, unless local law requires a different approach. Carried-over days that have not been forfeited are included in the payout. Sick leave is not paid out.

## 9. Questions

Questions about this policy should be sent to People Operations through the HR portal.
